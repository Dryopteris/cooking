## Yield
serves 6-8
optionally as chicken, breaded or grilled

## Ingredients 
- 12 oz. penne pasta
- ½ onion (¼ cup), minced
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 3 tbsp margarine, melted
- 8 oz. fresh mushrooms, sliced
- 15 oz. can artichoke hearts, drained & chopped
- 1 c whipping cream 
- 2-3 tbsp capers (drained) 
- ¼ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp pepper
- ¼ c parmesan cheese
- 2 tbsp chopped parsley

## Instructions
1. cook pasta, drain
1. cook onion and garlic in butter over med/hi heat 
1. add mushrooms, cook 5 minutes
1. add artichokes, cook 2 minutes
1. remove from skillet, set aside, keep warm
1. add whipping cream to skillet, boil stirring constantly until half volume
1. add artichoke mixture, capers, salt, pepper; stir well 
1. toss mixture with pasta, sprinkle with park and parsley
