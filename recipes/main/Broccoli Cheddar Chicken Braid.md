# Broccoli Cheddar Chicken Braid

## Ingredients
- 2 cups cheddar cheese
- 2 cans pillsbury crescent rolls
- 1 lb chicken
- 2 cups broccoli, frozen, steamed & chopped 
- ½ cup light mayonnaise
- 1 egg yolk (or the whole egg)
- fresh rosemary

## Instructions
1. cut the chicken into cubes, cook with oil
1. lightly steam the broccoli
1. Preheat oven to 375 °F
1. On a parchment paper lined cookie sheet, spread both cans of crescents length wise to form a long rectangle. Press seams to form a single layer of dough.
1. cut horizontal 1-inch Strips along the sides.
1. combine chicken, cheese, bloccoli, and mayonnaise 
1. spread mixture over the center of the dough.
1. food the dough strips over the top of the chicken mixture, alternating left and right to create a braid
1. brush braids with egg and sprinkle fresh rosemary
1. bake for 28-30 minutes or until golden brown
